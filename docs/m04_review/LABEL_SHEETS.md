# M04 label sheets (research aid, 2026-10-12)

CvD (cycle versus damage) asks one question about a slowdown: did the company go through an industry-wide trough and recover, or did it suffer damage of its own? Each episode takes one of six labels: CYCLICAL_TROUGH, CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE, STUMBLE, STRUCTURAL_DECLINE, NO_SLOWDOWN or AMBIGUOUS. Each red-flag row takes one answer: flag, clean or unsure. Every "Suggested label" below is a research suggestion only, and the owner decides. Leave a label blank to skip a row. Use AMBIGUOUS only when the evidence is genuinely mixed, not when you are unsure. These sheets were written from outside sources, without any engine output.

## CvD episodes

### mu_fy2012 — Micron Technology, FY2012
**Conclusion:** DRAM prices collapsed in 2011–12 because the industry had too much capacity and PC demand was weak. It hit every producer: Elpida went bankrupt and Micron lost money. Micron then bought Elpida and Rexchip, which consolidated the industry into three players. By FY2014 Micron was strongly profitable.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump was industry-wide, and Micron came out of it with more market share, not less.
**Facts:**
- FY2012 revenue was $8.2B, down 1% from FY2011. The net loss was $1.03B, against a $167M profit in FY2011. [The Register, 2012, https://www.theregister.co.uk/2012/10/01/micron_q4_fy2012/]
- Elpida filed for bankruptcy in February 2012. The CEO said Elpida held 15–20% of the memory market's production capacity. [Idaho Business Review, 2012, https://idahobusinessreview.com/?p=67478]
- Micron completed the Elpida and Rexchip acquisition on July 31, 2013. [Micron press release, 2013]
- FY2014 revenue was about $16.4B, with net income of about $3.0B (from memory, not checked against a source this session). [Micron FY2014 results, 2014]

### mu_fy2016 — Micron Technology, FY2016
**Conclusion:** Oversupply and weak PC demand pushed DRAM prices down through 2015–16, and Micron posted a small GAAP loss. There was a company-specific worry that Micron lagged Samsung on process technology. Even so, it set records in FY2017–18 as the cycle turned.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The downturn was industry-wide and the recovery was complete. If the owner gives weight to the technology gap against Samsung, CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE is a weaker alternative.
**Facts:**
- FY2016 revenue was $12.40B, against about $16.2B in FY2015. The GAAP net loss was $276M, while non-GAAP EPS was +$0.06. [Micron FY16 Q4 release via StorageNewsletter, 2016, https://www.storagenewsletter.com/?p=168438]
- In Q4 FY2016, DRAM prices fell about 6% while DRAM volumes rose about 20%. A 2016 restructuring plan was in place. [same]
- FY2017 revenue was about $20.3B, with net income of about $5.1B (from memory, not checked against a source this session). [Micron FY2017 results, 2017]
- FY2018 revenue was $30.4B, up 50%. [Micron FY18 Q4 release, 2018, https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2018/09/24/micron-fiscal-4q18-financial-results/]

### hal_2015_16 — Halliburton, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** Oil prices crashed and North American drilling collapsed across the industry. Halliburton's revenue fell by a third, and paying the $3.5B break fee when its Baker Hughes deal failed widened the loss. Its operations stayed intact, and revenue rebounded to $24B by 2018 as shale activity came back. It never got back to its 2014 size, but the whole oilfield-services sector shrank too.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump hit the whole industry, the damage came mostly from the cycle and the one-off break fee, and the business recovered with the cycle. AMBIGUOUS is defensible if you weigh the lower post-2014 peak.
**Facts:**
- 2016 revenue was $15.9B, down 33% from 2015. The 2016 net loss was about $5.8B, against a $0.67B loss in 2015. [Natural Gas World, 2017, naturalgasworld.com/halliburton-ends-2016-with-huge-loss-35515]
- Halliburton paid Baker Hughes a $3.5B termination fee on 4 May 2016 after the DOJ sued to block the merger. [Baker Hughes FY2016 10-K, 2017]
- In January 2016 the US rig count was the lowest since 1999. US and Canadian rig counts were down 48% year on year by mid-2016. [Oil & Gas Journal, 2016]
- 2018 revenue was $24.0B, up 16% from 2017. Operating income was $2.5B. [Halliburton IR, 2019, ir.halliburton.com/news-releases]

### nue_2015_16 — Nucor, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** US steel went through a slump driven by imports, with industry mills running at about 71% of capacity. Nucor stayed profitable and widened its margins in 2016. By 2018 it posted record earnings.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump was industry-wide and Nucor came through it with no lasting damage.
**Facts:**
- Imports took a record-matching 34% of the US steel market in 2015. [Nucor 2015 Annual Report, 2016]
- 2015 net earnings were only $80.7M, after a $153M impairment on a joint-venture investment. [Nucor 2016 Annual Report, 2017]
- Gross margin rose from 7% in 2015 to 12% in 2016, on 7% more tons shipped and cheaper raw materials. [Nucor 2016 Annual Report, 2017]
- Net sales rose 25% from $16.2B in 2016 to $20.3B in 2017. 2018 net earnings were a record $2.36B. [Nucor 2017 and 2018 Annual Reports; American Recycler, 2019]

### cat_2015_16 — Caterpillar, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** Mining and energy customers cut equipment spending sharply across the industry, and Caterpillar's sales fell from 2013 through 2016. It took restructuring charges and a loss year in 2016. Sales then recovered strongly, and 2018 brought record profit per share.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The downturn was in its end markets, and margins came back fully. The one bit of lasting damage is the mining-acquisition write-down, and revenue stayed below its 2012 peak for years.
**Facts:**
- 2016 sales and revenues were $38.5B, down 18%. Caterpillar booked a $67M loss, against a $2.5B profit in 2015. [For Construction Pros, 2017, forconstructionpros.com]
- The chairman expected 2016 to be the "fourth down year for sales and revenues". [IndustryWeek, 2016]
- Mining sales looked to have bottomed by early 2017. [Equipment FA, 2017, equipmentfa.com/news/6390]
- 2018 sales were $54.7B, up 20%, and profit per share was a record $10.26. [Caterpillar IR, 2019, caterpillar.com 4Q18 release]

### de_2015_16 — Deere, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** Low crop prices and falling farm incomes cut farm-equipment demand across the industry. Deere's sales and profit fell, but it stayed solidly profitable at the bottom. Sales recovered in 2017–2018, helped by buying Wirtgen.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump came from the farm cycle, Deere stayed profitable, and it lost no share or margin structure.
**Facts:**
- Fiscal 2016 revenue fell 8% to $26.6B. Net income was $1.52B, against $1.94B in 2015. [Deere FY2016 10-K, 2016]
- Deere expected 2016 US and Canadian farm-equipment industry sales to fall 15–20%, citing low commodity prices. [Deere FY2016 filings, 2016]
- Deere raised its 2016 profit forecast during the year, and the actual result beat the forecast. [Deere 10-Qs, 2016]
- Fiscal 2018 revenue rose 26% to $37.4B, with Wirtgen adding 12%. Net income was $2.37B even after a $704M tax-reform charge. [Deere FY2018 10-K, 2018]

### btu_2015_16 — Peabody Energy, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** The whole US coal industry collapsed: thermal demand fell as utilities switched to cheap gas, and seaborne met-coal prices crashed. Peabody also carried heavy debt from its 2011 Macarthur acquisition. It filed Chapter 11 in April 2016 and the old shares were cancelled with no value. The reorganized company made money in 2017–18 as coal recovered, but its old shareholders were wiped out.
**Suggested label:** CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE. The slump was industry-wide, but the bankruptcy did lasting harm to shareholders and the balance sheet. AMBIGUOUS is defensible because US thermal coal was also in long-term decline.
**Facts:**
- Peers Walter (Jul 2015), Alpha Natural (Aug 2015) and Arch (Jan 2016) also went bankrupt. [Reuters/WSJ coverage, 2015–16]
- Peabody's 2015 revenue was about $5.6B, down from about $6.8B in 2014, with a net loss of about $2B. [Peabody annual report, 2015]
- It filed Chapter 11 on Apr 13 2016 and emerged on Apr 3 2017. Debt fell by more than $5B, and the old BTUUQ shares were cancelled. [Peabody press release, 2017, https://peabodyenergy.investorroom.com/2017-04-03-Peabody-Emerges-From-Chapter-11-Protection-To-List-On-New-York-Stock-Exchange-Under-Ticker-Symbol-NYSE-BTU]
- The plan brought in about $3.5B of new debt and equity, and first-lien lenders were paid in full. [Davis Polk, 2017, https://www.davispolk.com/experience/peabody-energy-emergence-chapter-11-bankruptcy-0]

### chk_2015_16 — Chesapeake Energy, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** The 2015–16 oil and gas price collapse hit every producer. Chesapeake stood out for its heavy debt and expensive legacy contracts, and its stock fell below $2 in early 2016. Debt exchanges and asset sales kept it out of bankruptcy at the time. Its debt never really came down, though, and it filed Chapter 11 in June 2020 while gas peers survived.
**Suggested label:** CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE. The trough was industry-wide, but the balance-sheet damage never healed. It ended in bankruptcy, so this leans toward STRUCTURAL_DECLINE.
**Facts:**
- The 2015 net loss was $14.9B ($22.43 per share), driven by impairments, and revenue fell to about $12.8B from about $20.9B. [Chesapeake 2015 results, 2016]
- Henry Hub gas averaged about $2.6/MMBtu in 2015 and about $2.5 in 2016, the lowest in decades. [EIA, 2017]
- Debt of about $10B pushed it close to bankruptcy in early 2016, and it avoided filing through debt exchanges. [Motley Fool, 2020, https://www.fool.com/investing/2020/06/17/chesapeake-energy-was-once-the-second-biggest-natu.aspx]
- It filed Chapter 11 in June 2020 with debt near $9B and a plan to cut $7B. In May 2020 it had flagged a going-concern doubt. [CBS News, 2020, https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/oil-gas-fracking-chesapeake-energy-bankruptcy/]

### fcx_2015_16 — Freeport-McMoRan, 2015–2016
**Conclusion:** Copper prices bottomed in January 2016, and oil fell at the same time. That hurt Freeport twice, because it had borrowed heavily in 2013 to buy oil and gas businesses. It survived by selling Tenke, a 13% stake in Morenci and its oil and gas assets, and by issuing about 40% more shares. From 2017 it recovered with copper, but it kept the smaller asset base and the larger share count.
**Suggested label:** CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE. The copper slump was industry-wide, but the forced asset sales and dilution were permanent.
**Facts:**
- Copper hit a low of about $1.94/lb in January 2016, and the 2016 average was $2.20/lb, down 12% from 2015. [USGS, 2016, https://apps.usgs.gov/minerals-information-archives/copper/mis/mis-201612-coppe.pdf]
- Net losses were about $12.2B in 2015 and $4.2B in 2016, mostly from oil and gas impairments. [FCX annual results, 2016–17]
- Asset sales raised $6.6B in 2016, including $2.65B for Tenke. A $1.5B share sale issued 116.5M shares, and net debt fell by $8.4B to about $12B. [FCX Q4 2016 release, 2017, https://investors.fcx.com/investors/news-releases/news-release-details/2017/Freeport-McMoRan-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Year-Ended-December31-2016-Results/default.aspx]
- The share count rose from about 1.04B at end-2014 to about 1.45B at end-2016. [FCX 10-K, 2016]

### amd_2012_15 — Advanced Micro Devices, 2012–2015
**Conclusion:** The PC market was shrinking in these years, but AMD's slide was mostly its own doing. Its Bulldozer-era CPUs could not compete with Intel, and it also lost GPU share to Nvidia. Only game-console chips held up. The Zen/Ryzen launch in 2017 brought it back, so the damage was eventually fixed, but by its own new products rather than by a PC recovery.
**Suggested label:** AMBIGUOUS, leaning STUMBLE. The PC slump was real, but the share loss was company-specific and was fixed later by Zen. CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE is also defensible.
**Facts:**
- Revenue was $5.42B in 2012, $5.30B in 2013 and $5.51B in 2014, then $3.99B in 2015 (−28%, mainly lower client processor sales). [AMD IR, 2016, https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/662/amd-reports-2015-fourth-quarter-and-annual-results; GamersNexus, 2014]
- 2015 GAAP net loss was $660M (2014: −$403M). Gross margin fell to 27%. [AMD IR, 2016]
- x86 PC share fell from 48.4% (Q1 2006) to 23.2% (Q3 2014). [PassMark via Motley Fool, 2014]
- In 2014, Computing & Graphics revenue fell 16% while semi-custom console chips rose 51%. [GamersNexus, 2014, https://gamersnexus.net/industry/1789-amd-financial-situation-2014]
- Ryzen launched in 2017, and AMD then took share for years. The 2017 figures are from memory and were not checked in this research. [AMD annual reports]

### jcp_fy2012 — J.C. Penney, FY2012
**Conclusion:** Ron Johnson's switch to everyday pricing with no coupons drove core shoppers away while other retailers were growing. He was replaced in April 2013. Sales steadied at a much lower level and never got back to the pre-2012 base.
**Suggested label:** STRUCTURAL_DECLINE. The cause was company-specific and was never fixed. STUMBLE is the alternative if you count the 2013–15 stabilisation as a fix.
**Facts:**
- FY2012 sales fell 24.8% to $12.985B, the lowest since 1987. Comparable-store sales fell 25.2%. [JCK, 2013, https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/jc-penney-lost-nearly-1-billion-in-2012/]
- Net loss was $985M ($4.49/share), and $552M of it came in Q4. [JCK, 2013, same URL]
- Johnson admitted the "fair and square" pricing was a mistake and brought coupons back. He was then replaced by Mike Ullman. [Retail Dive, 2013, https://www.retaildive.com/news/jcpenney-nosedived-in-2012-whats-next/104993/]
- Hindsight: 2019 sales were $10.7B, against $19.9B in 2007. Losses from 2011 to 2019 totalled $4.2B. [Fortune, 2020, https://fortune.com/2020/05/15/jc-penney-files-for-bankruptcy-close-stores]

### gpro_2016 — GoPro, 2016
**Conclusion:** This was company-specific: the action-camera market was saturating and phones were replacing dedicated cameras. Sales fell, the Karma drone was recalled, and GoPro made repeated layoffs. Revenue never got back to its 2015 peak, GoPro left the drone business in 2018, and it shrank further after that.
**Suggested label:** STRUCTURAL_DECLINE. This was not an industry cycle, and the lost scale never came back.
**Facts:**
- 2016 revenue was $1.19B, down 27% from about $1.62B in 2015. [GoPro 10-K, 2017; bicycleretailer, 2017, https://www.bicycleretailer.com/north-america/2017/02/02/gopro-misses-q4-revenue-target-posts-115m-loss]
- Net loss was about $419M. Some outlets report $373M, so check the 10-K for the GAAP figure. [Business Insider / PetaPixel, 2017]
- Camera shipments fell nearly 50% through the first three quarters of 2016, and the stock fell about 52%. [Business Insider, 2017]
- The Karma drone was recalled in November 2016. GoPro cut 15% of staff and its president left. [Quartz, 2016, https://qz.com/849061/]

### shld_2012_17 — Sears Holdings, 2012–2017
**Conclusion:** Revenue roughly halved and losses ran above $1B a year. The company closed about a third of its stores and sold or spun off assets (Lands' End, part of Sears Canada) to fund itself, while the wider retail sector held up. It disclosed going-concern doubt in 2017 and filed for bankruptcy in October 2018.
**Suggested label:** STRUCTURAL_DECLINE. The slide was company-specific and ended in bankruptcy.
**Facts:**
- Revenue fell from $39.85B in 2012 to $22.13B in 2016. [Motley Fool, 2017, https://www.fool.com/investing/2017/07/10/heres-how-far-sears-holdings-has-fallen-in-5-years.aspx]
- Net losses were $0.93B, $1.36B, $1.68B, $1.1B and $2.22B for 2012 to 2016. [Motley Fool, 2017, same URL]
- The March 2017 10-K stated "substantial doubt" about its ability to continue as a going concern. [Fortune, 2017, https://fortune.com/2017/03/22/sears-bankruptcy-2/amp]
- It filed for Chapter 11 on 15 October 2018, just before a $134M debt payment was due. [CBS News, 2018, https://www.cbsnews.com/news/sears-closing-another-142-stores-bankruptcy-filing-liquidation-sales-coming-holiday-shopping]

### rshcq_2012_14 — RadioShack, 2012–2014
**Conclusion:** RadioShack's mall electronics model lost out to online retailers and big-box stores, and its revenue depended heavily on low-margin phone sales. It posted 11 straight quarterly losses, warned of possible bankruptcy in 2014 and filed for Chapter 11 in February 2015.
**Suggested label:** STRUCTURAL_DECLINE. The slide was company-specific and ended in bankruptcy.
**Facts:**
- FY2013 revenue fell 10% to $3.43B, and the net loss widened to $400.2M from $139.4M. [Boston Globe, 2014, https://www.bostonglobe.com/business/2014/03/05/radioshack-closing-stores-troubles-grow/9yYMmKJl2vsFl65TYxPzaN/story.html]

- It filed for Chapter 11 on 5 February 2015 with $1.2B of assets against $1.39B of debts. [Fortune, 2015, https://fortune.com/2015/02/05/radioshack-bankruptcy-filing]

### mu_fy2019_20 — Micron Technology, FY2019–FY2020
**Conclusion:** After the 2018 peak, memory prices fell because customers had built up inventory, and demand from China was weak. Micron's revenue declined, but the company stayed solidly profitable. It recovered in FY2021–22.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump was industry-wide, Micron remained profitable throughout, and it showed no lasting damage. A label of NO_SLOWDOWN would understate a revenue drop of about 30% from the peak.
**Facts:**
- Revenue went from $30.39B in FY2018 to $23.41B in FY2019 and $21.44B in FY2020. [Micron FY20 Q4 release via StorageNewsletter, 2020, https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2020/09/30/micron-fiscal-4q20-financial-results-results/]
- Operating income fell from $7.38B to $3.00B. FY2020 GAAP net income was $2.69B. [same]
- Operating cash flow fell from $13.19B to $8.31B. [same]
- In Q4 FY2020, revenue was up 24% from a year earlier, as recovery was already under way. FY2022 revenue was about $30.8B. [same; Micron FY23 release, 2023]

### mu_fy2023 — Micron Technology, FY2023
**Conclusion:** This was the worst memory downturn in more than a decade. Demand collapsed after the pandemic and customers held too much inventory. Revenue halved, Micron took inventory write-downs and posted large losses. China also restricted Micron products in 2023. Micron recovered strongly in FY2024–25, helped by high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump was industry-wide and Micron recovered to record revenue. The China ban was a contained, company-specific loss. If the owner weighs it heavily, CYCLE_PLUS_DAMAGE is possible but weak.
**Facts:**
- FY2023 revenue was $15.54B, against $30.76B in FY2022. The GAAP net loss was $5.83B. [Micron FY23 Q4 release via StorageNewsletter, 2023, https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2023/09/28/micron-fiscal-4q23-financial-results/]
- Write-downs of inventory to net realizable value totalled $1.83B, with a net impact of $0.99B. [Micron FY2023 10-K, 2023]
- In May 2023, China's cybersecurity regulator (CAC) barred operators of critical infrastructure from buying Micron products (from memory, not checked against a source this session). [Reuters, 2023]
- Revenue was about $25.1B in FY2024 and about $37.4B in FY2025, a record, with HBM ramping. These figures are from memory and were not checked against a source this session. [Micron FY24/FY25 results]

### wdc_fy2023 — Western Digital, FY2023
**Conclusion:** FY2023 caught Western Digital in what management called the worst memory and storage downturn in more than a decade. Both flash and hard drives were hit, and peers fell just as hard: Seagate's revenue dropped about 37% and Micron's about 49%. Revenue had recovered by FY2025, the flash business was spun off as SanDisk in February 2025, and AI demand for high-capacity drives brought record margins.
**Suggested label:** CYCLICAL_TROUGH. The slump was industry-wide, and the company recovered with the cycle. The one caveat is that it raised convertible financing, which caused some dilution.
**Facts:**
- FY2023 revenue was $12.3B, down 34%. Gross margin fell to 15.3% from 31.3%, and the net loss was $1.7B. [Business Wire, 2023, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230731170808/en]
- Flash prices fell about 20% blended in a single quarter (Q2), and $211M of underutilization charges came in Q4. [StorageNewsletter, 2023, https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2023/02/01/wd-fiscal-2q23-financial-results/]
- Management said the low-cost flash from its Kioxia joint venture kept product gross margin positive. [Motley Fool transcript, 2023, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2023/08/01/western-digital-wdc-q4-2023-earnings-call-transcri]
- It issued $900M of convertible preferred stock to Apollo and Elliott in January 2023. [WDC press release, 2023]

### intc_2022_25 — Intel, 2022–2025
**Conclusion:** The PC and server downturn of 2022–23 hit the whole industry. Intel also kept losing data-center and PC share to AMD and Arm while its foundry push burned cash. The 2024 loss was its worst since 1986, and in 2025 it brought in a new CEO, layoffs and a 10% US government stake. Intel did not recover with the cycle: AI spending lifted its peers but not Intel.

**Facts:**

### nvta_2022_23 — Invitae, 2022–2023

After a 2022 restructuring and deep cost cuts, it filed for Chapter 11 in February 2024. Labcorp bought its assets in May 2024. Rival genetic-testing labs survived the 2022 funding downturn, so this was the company's own failure, not the industry's.

### bbby_2019_23 — Bed Bath & Beyond, 2019–2023
**Conclusion:** Sales fell for years as Amazon, Walmart, Target and IKEA took customers. Under the 2019 turnaround plan, the company replaced national brands with its own private labels, which left shelves short of key products. Sales then collapsed in 2022, and it filed for Chapter 11 in April 2023 and was liquidated. Home-goods spending boomed in 2020–21, so the industry was not in a slump.
**Suggested label:** STRUCTURAL_DECLINE. It lost share while the category grew, and the company was liquidated.
**Facts:**
- FY2019 revenue fell 7% to $11.16B, and Q3 FY2019 comparable sales fell 8.3%. [Benzinga, 2020, https://benzinga.com/news/earnings/20/01/15100052/bed-bath-beyonds-stock-plunges-with-hard-times-on-the-horizon]
- Q1 FY2022 revenue fell 25% and comparable sales fell 23%, and the CEO was dismissed. [NBC LA/CNBC, 2022, https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/business/money-report/bed-bath-beyond-announces-ceo-is-leaving-as-it-sharply-misses-quarterly-expectations/2927111/]
