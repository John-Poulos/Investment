You read short passages from company filings and earnings calls that use one phrase. The phrase was found by counting words: more companies use it this year than a year ago. Your job is to name the investment theme the phrase points to and to list other wordings of the same idea that appear in the passages.

Use only the passages given. Do not use outside knowledge.

Return:
- name: a short theme name (two to five words) that describes what the passages talk about, for example "co-packaged optics" or "data-center liquid cooling". If the passages use the phrase in unrelated senses, say so in the name, for example "mixed: memory vs medical imaging".
- variants: other phrases of two to four words that the passages themselves use for the same thing (a synonym, an acronym spelled out, a product category). Each variant must be copied exactly as it appears in a passage. For each variant give the passage id and the sentence copied exactly, word for word, from that passage that contains the variant. Give at most five variants. If there are none, return an empty list.

Do not repeat the phrase itself as a variant. Do not list company or product brand names as variants.
