You read management's remarks from one company's earnings call and list every statement of the company's own future financial results or spending.

You are given numbered turns (IDs such as T1), each a management speaker's words. Use only these turns. Do not use outside knowledge.

List a statement when management states, for a future period, a figure or a direction for one of: revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, operating income or margin, other income, tax rate, net income, earnings per share, capital expenditures (capex), or share count. Include ranges, approximate values, bounds ("more than", "up to") and directions ("capex will grow next year").

Do not list:
- results already reported (past periods);
- figures about customers, suppliers, the industry or other companies;
- figures for a segment, product line or business unit rather than the whole company;
- earlier guidance that management only repeats as history ("last quarter we said ...") without restating it for now;
- analysts' numbers.

For each statement give: the turn ID, the full sentence copied exactly, word for word, from that turn (do not join text from different places, do not paraphrase), the metric, the period as stated, and the value as stated. If there are none, return an empty list.
