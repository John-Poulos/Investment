How to resolve review items — what each choice does, with worked examples

What your choice changes

ActionEffect on the program
Enter guidanceAdds a real row to the guidance ledger (marked owner-entered). It appears in the Guidance tab, takes part in raised/lowered comparisons and Gemini reason analysis, and will be scored against actual results (M11, R19). Survives re-scans and overrides the parser for that metric and period.
Dismiss / acknowledgeChanges no data. Records your note and stops re-scans from raising the same sentence again. Notes also show which wording the parser misses.
Keep stored / keep otherChooses which of two parsed values stays in the ledger.
Guidance check: correct / wrongA stored row from the weekly random sample or a plausibility flag. Correct keeps it and counts toward the parser's measured precision; Wrong (with a reason) removes it and keeps it out of later scans. Either answer becomes a label every new parser version is re-checked against.
Guidance check: not guidance / missedA sentence the parser set aside (weekly sample) or one Gemini read as guidance that the code did not store. Missed records a gap for the next parser build (enter the value by hand from the sentence's review item if you need it now); Not guidance confirms the skip.
Leave openNothing is lost. Use this for real information the program cannot store yet; a later module can pick it up from the queue.
Reopen (resolved items)Undo. Returns the item to the queue; an entered value is removed from the ledger.

Rules for a good entry (so the ledger stays trustworthy)

  1. Enter only forward guidance the sentence itself states — never a reported result ("revenue was…").
  2. Use only numbers in the sentence. Do not convert a growth rate into dollars or combine sentences. The form warns when a number is not in the sentence; treat that warning as a stop sign.
  3. Period as the company labels it: FY2026, FY2026 Q3, or FY2026 remaining. Fiscal years can differ from calendar years (NVIDIA, Micron, Marvell).
  4. Basis stays "unspecified" unless the sentence says GAAP or non-GAAP.
  5. Unit and scale: "$5 billion" → unit USD, low 5, scale billions. "52% to 53%" → unit %, low 52, high 53. Leave High empty for a single number.
  6. If the metric is not in the list (bookings, growth rate, deliveries, dividends), do not force it into a similar metric — leave the item open.
  7. When unsure, leave it open. A missing value is safer than a wrong one.

M02 proposals (themes, candidates, links)

KindActions and their effect
New theme / new phraseAccept adds it to theme counting from now on (its discovery date is kept). Reject stops it; it returns only if its distinct filers double or money-flow evidence appears, with your note shown. Leave open if you want another season of data.
How the seasonal pass found it: your watchlist companies started using the phrase this year (compared with their own filings a year earlier), and more filers of all kinds now use it than a year ago, beyond chance (p ≤ 0.10). Judge it from the three sample passages and the industry list: "co-packaged optics" — 9 → 21 companies, mostly chips/computers, passages about switch optics → Accept as a phrase for Custom silicon & optics. "HBM" with samples about hepatic, brain or memory — the acronym means different things in different industries → Reject, note "acronym clash; keep 'high bandwidth memory'". "agentic ai" with no theme fit → Accept creates a new theme named after the phrase (rename later); Reject if it is a buzzword you do not want to follow. A "crowding" tag (new funds naming it) argues for caution, not rejection. A phrase that is only new wording of an accounting or legal rule ("pillar two", "cybersecurity incident") → Reject, note "regulatory wording".
Candidate companyAccept puts it on the Tracked list (filings, XBRL, earnings releases and call transcripts; a few requests a quarter). Reject as above. Prefer candidates with a substantive mention (business section or numbers), not only risk factors.
Examples: a photonics maker whose MD&A says "co-packaged optics orders reached $12 million" → Accept. A former mining shell renamed two months ago, float $18M, S-3 filed after its first "quantum computing" mention, business section says it "intends to develop" software → the flags are there for this case: Reject unless the passage shows real products or customers (S116–S118). A bank naming the theme only as a risk is not proposed (watch tier, Themes page).
Customer–supplier linkConfirm when the quote names both companies and says who supplies whom; Confirm, direction flipped when the quote supports the reverse; Reject otherwise. A missing link means unknown, not "no link". The item states a claimed direction ("Micron supplies NVIDIA (L2 wording)"). Examples: "Sales to NVIDIA accounted for 12% of our revenue" in Micron's 10-K → Confirm; "we rely on TSMC to manufacture our GPUs" in NVIDIA's 10-K, claimed "NVIDIA supplies TSMC" → Confirm, direction flipped; "we compete with NVIDIA" or a product that merely runs on NVIDIA GPUs → Reject. A contract (L3) often states no direction: confirm only if the passage says who buys. A confirmed supplier outside your lists is proposed as a candidate; a tracked one gets a promotion suggestion.
Financing or equity linkA sentence naming another company with equity, warrant, backstop, financing or joint-venture wording (D-M02-41). The item states a claim ("NVIDIA invests in Intel"). Confirm when the quote says who puts money or equity into whom; Confirm, direction flipped when it says the reverse; change the kind in the list beside the button if the code read it wrong; Reject otherwise. Kinds: K1 equity purchase · K2 equity granted to a customer · K3 revenue backstop · K4 customer financing, loans, guarantees · K6 joint venture / equity-method stake. Examples: Marvell's 10-Q "sale of $2.0 billion of Series A preferred stock to NVIDIA" → Confirm, K1, NVIDIA → Marvell, $2.0B; Intel's 10-K "issue and sell 215 million shares of our common stock to NVIDIA … $5.0 billion" → Confirm, K1, NVIDIA → Intel; CoreWeave's 10-K "NVIDIA agreed to purchase any unsold capacity" → Confirm, K3, NVIDIA backstops CoreWeave; hypothetical: a supplier's 8-K "issued to Amazon a warrant to purchase up to … shares", vesting with Amazon's purchases → Confirm, kind K2, supplier → Amazon; a joint venture with a competitor named only in a market overview, or a product name ("NVIDIA DGX") → Reject. Confirmed links feed the loop flag when the same pair has a confirmed supply link.
13F issuer to mapA 13F holding whose issuer name matched no single SEC company. Enter the ticker (or CIK) and Map to this company — every holding of that issuer (all filers, all quarters) then resolves. Dismiss for lines that are not a company you want linked (funds, SPAC units, warrants listed under a trust). Example: an unresolved line "XYZ HOLDINGS WTS" → map to XYZ's ticker if it is the operating company's warrant, else Dismiss; Alphabet's "PARABILIS MEDICINES INC" → map if it has a ticker, otherwise Dismiss.
Financing record failed a checkA 13F or 13D/13G record held before storing: the filing's rows do not add up, the shares are more than 10× the prior quarter's, or value ≠ shares × the closing price by more than 25%. Keep when the filing is right (NVIDIA's Intel stake after its $5B purchase is a real jump); Wrong — remove the record keeps it out of later runs (undo on the Runs page). Every stored record also has a "wrong?" button on the Money flow tab.
News on accepted themes (weekly digest)One item per week, counted as 1 of the 10 (D-M02-58): distinct news events with a theme phrase, grouped by theme — the company's own IR feed or 8-K release (grade A), or trade press / a known outlet naming a watchlist or tracked company (B) — plus companies naming an accepted theme for the first time in their own documents, and Federal Register final rules naming a theme phrase in their title or abstract and bills that became law (D-M02-61). Noted changes no data; leave it open to come back to it. Examples: an NVIDIA IR headline "… AI accelerator platform" also filed as an 8-K and carried by Reuters → one event with three sources; an EE Times story "Micron ramps HBM4" → an event for MU under HBM; a Yahoo opinion piece "Is HBM a bubble?" → counted only (grade C); Intel's new 10-Q naming "co-packaged optics" when no stored Intel document did before → a first mention (open the section and judge whether it is substance or a passing word). News is evidence, never a signal on its own.
IR feed foundA watchlist or tracked company had no feed in config/news.toml, and a feed on its investor site passed the checks (3+ recent entries linking to the company's own domain, D-M02-59). Confirm when the headlines shown are this company's press releases: the feed is polled on every run as grade A. Reject when they are not (another company, a blog of job posts, an empty archive): nothing is polled and the company's News tab says so — add the right address to the config file if you know it. Example: "SpaceX Press Releases" at ir.spacex.com/rss/pressrelease.aspx with "SpaceX Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results" → Confirm.
Financing alert (weekly digest)Informational: a new loop flag on confirmed links, or a credit series rising (allowance or guarantee ratio two quarters in a row; days sales in receivables up more than 10%). Noted changes no data; read the series on the company's Money flow tab. A hypothesis, not a signal (D-M02-41).

M04 filing events, restatements and ratings (Part 3)

KindActions and their effect, with worked examples
Filing eventAn 8-K item or late-filing notice M04 reads (D-M04-37, -15): 1.03 bankruptcy and 2.04 acceleration fail the survival gate (Part 4a); 3.01 fails on delisting or a missed cure and warns on a cure-period deficiency; 4.02 non-reliance warns and marks rows "inputs under non-reliance" (adds are not blocked, D-M04-21); a 4.01 auditor change within 12 months of a restatement escalates; 2.06 impairments and NT filings ask for your reading. Resolve (with a note) clears a fail or warn; a 2.04 clears only once a later periodic filing also exists; a 1.03 never clears by itself. Noted closes the item and leaves the state.
Examples: Invitae's 8-K of 2024-02-14 (1.03 bankruptcy and 2.04 acceleration) → leave both failing; Noted, note "Chapter 11" — the gate keeps failing, as it should. A Nasdaq notice "the Company is not in compliance … has 180 calendar days to regain compliance" → deficiency, warn: Noted while the period runs; a later 8-K "regained compliance" clears it by itself, or Resolve with "regained per press release of …". A 4.01 four months after a 4.02, new auditor named → escalated: read the 4.01 for disagreements; Noted keeps the warning. An NT 10-Q followed by the 10-Q a week later clears by itself (no action needed). Infleqtion's 3.01 of 2026-02-03 moving its listing to the NYSE is information and opens no item.
Restatement step not explainedA value changed between two filings, and what is left after the ordered labels (standard adoption, discontinued operations, segment recast, tag switch, rounding, NCI share, restricted cash, de-SPAC) is above both 0.5% of the old value and $0.1M (D-M04-16). Open the changing filing (link in the item) and read the note on the change. Explained when it is a recast, adoption or reclassification (the step stops counting in restate_delta); Error when the filing corrects an error ("revision of previously issued financial statements", "immaterial error"): it then counts as little r in restatement.
Examples: GE's FY2016 operating cash flow moved +$1,404M in the FY2018 10-K; the text quotes $1,400M for the ASU 2016-15 reclassification, $4M is left on a −$244M base → Explained (the adoption; the $4M is rounding across lines). Infleqtion's Q1 2026 revenue +$446K and net loss −$544K, with the 10-Q text "revision of previously issued financial statements … error" → Error. Rite Aid's FY2017 revenue 32,845 → 22,928 after the store sale is labelled discontinued operations by the code and never reaches you.
Credit-rating actionA sentence in an 8-K or earnings release stating a downgrade, negative outlook or watch (S3.7). Information only (no weight); Noted changes no data. Example: Intel, August 2025: long-term rating lowered from BBB+ to BBB → Noted; read it with the gate's runway and maturity wall (Part 4a). A sentence like "a downgrade could increase our borrowing costs" is hypothetical and never raised.

Mentions measure attention, not money: weigh a proposal by its evidence grade and money flow first, then breadth growth. A theme that is already crowded (many new funds) is a warning, not a reason to buy.

M04 survival gate (Part 4a)

KindActions and their effect, with worked examples
Survival gate turned fail or incomplete (m04_gate_fail, alert, uncapped)Opens when the gate turns fail (runway below 6 quarters for a cash burner, 12-month debt above liquidity, an open 8-K 1.03 / 2.04 / 3.01 fail) or incomplete (cash or two quarters of OCF missing, an overdue filing, a foreign filer's inputs older than 16 months, inconsistent inputs). Noted keeps the state; Inputs wrong (note required) records what is wrong.
Examples: Rivian Q2 2026 on cash only: 5,310 ÷ 962 burn a quarter (last two quarters) = 5.5 quarters → fail. Its 536M ABL is untagged; confirm it on the gate panel ("Add confirmed credit line", maturing beyond 12 months) and the runway reads 6.1 → warn. → Noted before, or Inputs wrong "ABL not counted" and add the line. Boeing FY2019: (10,030 + 6,400 of credit maturing beyond 12 months) ÷ 2,778 = 5.9 → fail; the 364-day 3.2B line ending October 2020 does not count → Noted.
Capex-funded runway below 6 quarters (m04_runway, priority 1)OCF is positive but FCF on total capex is negative and the runway is below 6 quarters: the gate warns and never fails; while open, rule-3 adds are allowed and rule-7 strength is blocked (D-M04-38). Capex can be cut (allow strength) when the build-out can be slowed; Keep blocked otherwise.
Example: Intel FY2024: liquidity 22,062 ÷ burn 3,914 a quarter = 5.6 quarters with OCF +8,288; capex fell 39% the next year → Capex can be cut.
Gate input to map (m04_gate_input, operational, uncapped)A non-financing input is missing or untagged (capex, cash, OCF; financing tags are mapped in M02). Map tag with a stored tag (taxonomy:Tag) makes it this company's input from the next recompute; Not reported keeps the labelled fallback.
Example: Infleqtion after the de-SPAC: capex untagged → OCF-only runway 93.6 quarters (477 ÷ 5.1) → Map tag if its 10-Q tags capex under another name, else Not reported.
Distress score while the gate passes (m04_score_gap, priority 1)Z (manufacturers) or Z'' in the distress zone while the gate reads pass or warn (S4.8). The score is a warning only, never a gate input (D-M04-31). Noted, or Look closer with what to check.
Example: Boeing FY2025 Z 1.53 (below 1.81) with a passing gate (runway 48.7 quarters) → Look closer "negative retained earnings after losses; liquidity strong". GlobalFoundries 2.07 and Intel 2.00 are grey and open nothing.

Survival items flush first (priority 1); gate fails and gate inputs are outside the weekly cap of 5, the runway and score-gap reviews count in it (D-M04-71).

M04 survival gate text (Part 4b)

KindActions and their effect, with worked examples
Fail-class text (m04_gate_fail, alert, uncapped)A sentence in the current report that fails the gate: the auditor's going-concern paragraph, management's "substantial doubt exists" (also a present-tense risk factor), IFRS "a material uncertainty exists", a breach or event of default the company states under its own debt agreements and nobody waived, forbearance, debt classified as current because of a breach, acceleration, delisting (D-M04-34). Noted keeps it; Inputs wrong (note) says the code misread the sentence, and it stops acting on the gate.
Examples: Invitae's Q3 2023 10-Q, "management concluded that substantial doubt exists about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern" → Noted (98 days later it filed for Chapter 11). Fisker's FY2023 10-K: the auditor's paragraph and a forbearance agreement to 2024-05-01 → Noted. A risk factor "an event of default could accelerate our debt" never reaches you: it is hypothetical; nor do indemnities ("losses arising from a breach of representations or covenants"), lawsuits ("breach of the implied covenant of good faith") or facility descriptions ("contain customary events of default"). When the text is the only trigger, this is the one alert; Part 4a's "gate turned fail" alert opens only when other triggers fail the gate too.
Going-concern wording to read (m04_gc_text, priority 1)Doubt raised but alleviated by management's plans, a critical audit matter about the going-concern assessment without a going-concern paragraph, IFRS "material uncertainty" not stated as existing, or hypothetical wording ("could raise substantial doubt") while the runway is below 8 quarters. The first three warn the gate while their report is current; hypothetical wording never changes the gate. Noted keeps the class; Treat as fail when the plans depend on financing not yet obtained; Misread (note) when the sentence is not what the code says.
Examples: Microvast FY2025 10-K, plans "are probable to alleviate the substantial doubt" → Noted (warn stays while the report is current); if the plan is an unclosed raise → Treat as fail. Riot FY2025 risk factor "could raise substantial doubt about our ability to continue as a going concern" with the runway at 5 quarters → Noted. Infleqtion's FY2025 10-K "was alleviated due to the closing of the Business Combination" covers the shell year (period 2025-12-31 before the 2026-02-13 close): excluded, never queued; a later 10-Q repeating "alleviated due to the closing" warns with one review (the same sentence in later reports is the same item).
Covenant waived or reset, or a listing notice (m04_covenant, priority 1)A past breach or default the lenders waived or that was cured ("in compliance … however, … had a technical default"), covenant relief (a "covenant relief period", a raised limit, suspended covenants, an amendment to avoid a breach; a routine amendment that conforms a covenant is none), or a listing-standard deficiency with a cure period: the gate warns while the report is current. Same actions as above.
Examples: Universal Technical Institute Q3 FY2025, quick ratio 0.62 against 0.65 required, "obtained a waiver from the lender for this covenant violation" → Noted (a trip wire at a healthy firm). Albemarle's Second Amendment (EX-10.1, Q3 2024 10-Q): a Covenant Relief Period to the Q2 2026 certificate, buybacks barred → Noted; read it with the runway and the maturity wall. GlobalFoundries' 20-F risk factor "Although our lenders agreed to waive the defaults, …" → Noted or Misread; its next 20-F repeating the sentence opens no new item and carries your answer. Boeing FY2023 "we were in compliance with the covenants for our debt and credit facilities" opens nothing.

Only the company's current report (latest periodic filing with stored text, plus later 6-Ks) acts on the gate; older sentences are history. The first scan of each company is a baseline: shown on the Gate text block, queued only when filed in the last 12 months or a current fail (D-M04-71).
